The **Sicalo Lab** studies parasite drug resistance, integrating field parasitology with phenotypic and genetic approaches to support sustainable parasite control.

We investigate how genetic variation, parasite biology, and management practices interact to shape treatment outcomes in livestock and other animal hosts. Our work combines field-derived samples, phenotypic drug-response data, and genomic information through collaborative, interdisciplinary research.
